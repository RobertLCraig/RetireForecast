## What you need to know
- The model raises the service charge smoothly and has no one-off major-works bill.
- A section 20 major-works bill is legally enforceable, cannot be put off, and arrives as one demand.
- The survivor's spare money is small each month, so one such bill could wipe out years of it. It falls only on the keep-the-flat plans.
- Without real figures the property reviewer assumes two big bills: one in the 2030s, one in the 2040s.
- Insurance, safety compliance and energy inside service charges have risen faster than inflation since 2019. Three years of accounts show whether that is true here.
- An unresolved building-safety position is a common reason flats sell slowly. A qualifying leaseholder's share of safety costs is capped.
