# TIME LIMITED: the park-home heat-pump grant

## What I need from you
Decide whether a park home is still a live option. If it is, ask one specific park three
questions before **31 March 2027**, when the grant closes:

1. Is heat-pump and solar installation permitted on the pitch? Pass: consent in writing.
2. Own MPAN or site-owner electricity resale? This alone decides whether solar can earn export
   income.
3. Will the roof take panels?

If it is not a live option, say so and this card is dropped. Leaving it unanswered past January
2027 answers it by default, which is the worst of the three outcomes because the GBP 9,000 is
simply forgone.

## Why
Only relevant if a park home is on the table, but it expires. A main-residence park home on a
**residential-licensed** site now qualifies for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme (the EPC blocker was
removed 28 April 2026), and replacing **LPG** off gas grid draws **GBP 9,000 until 31 March 2027**.
Not modelled. See PLAN-park-home "Heat pumps and solar".

## Options
1. **Park homes are on the table.** Then ask any specific park about heat-pump and solar consent,
   the electricity supply arrangement (own MPAN or site-owner resale, which decides whether solar
   can earn export income), and whether the roof takes panels. Model the GBP 9,000.
2. **Park homes are not on the table.** Drop this card and the four park-home scenarios stay as
   exploratory only.

## Recommendation
Decide which before March 2027, not because the modelling is hard but because the money has a
deadline and the questions have to be asked of a specific site. If park homes are still live in
January 2027 and this is unanswered, it has effectively answered itself.
