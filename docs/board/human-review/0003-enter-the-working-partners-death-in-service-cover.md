# Enter the working partner's death-in-service cover

## Why
Found 2026-07-31. The cover is confirmed in force (PLAN-adviser-parity "Decisions resolved" #4)
but no stored scenario records any, so the new "If one of you died" panel shows GBP 0 of cover
for them.

## Options
1. **Enter it now**, at builder step 1 → the employed person → "Death-in-service cover from
   their employer" (a multiple of salary, or a fixed sum).
2. **Leave it.** The modelling already shows the working partner's death is *not* the
   household's protection risk; the retired partner's is, and no employer cover exists for them.

## Recommendation
Enter it, but do not expect it to move the answer. It is cheap, it makes the panel honest, and
the protection gap it closes is the one that was never the risk. The finding worth carrying
forward is the other half: the retired partner is uncovered and that is where the exposure sits.
