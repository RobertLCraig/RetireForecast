# Confirm the State Pension figure, then make three benefits calls

## What I need from you

**Four things. The first one decides the other three.**

1. Get the working partner's State Pension forecast from `gov.uk/check-state-pension` and tell me
   the exact weekly figure and the number of qualifying years.
2. Book a free benefits check - Age UK on 0800 678 1602.
3. Check the council tax bill for the disabled band reduction. It is **not** means-tested.
4. Confirm the older partner's disability award splits into its care and mobility rates, and give
   me both.

---

**On 1.** The whole benefits picture in this plan turns on a few pounds a week. If she is on the
full new State Pension she sits just **above** the Pension Credit line and gets nothing - no
Pension Credit, and none of the help it passports: mortgage interest support, council tax
reduction, warm home discount, free TV licence at 75, cold weather payments, help with NHS costs. A
few pounds the other way and the whole package switches on. The tool currently models a large
lifetime Pension Credit award that is probably zero, and that figure is one of the two pillars of
the argument for keeping the flat.

Pass is a printed or screenshotted forecast with the weekly amount and the qualifying years. If she
is short of the full record, ask whether voluntary contributions are still open - the payback is not
the pension, it is that dropping below the line re-opens everything else.

**On 2.** Not because a check will find Pension Credit today. Because it gets the claim, the
carer's underlying entitlement and the council tax reductions **on file before they are needed**.

**On 3.** This is the only item on the list that pays immediately. It needs a qualifying feature -
an extra bathroom, a room used for the disabled person's needs, or space to use a wheelchair
indoors - and it drops the bill a whole band.

**On 4.** Card 0050 cannot be built without the split. The two components are treated completely
differently in a care assessment and only one of them stops in a funded placement.

**Two corrections to the existing benefits file, while you are at it.** The advice not to apply for
Attendance Allowance for the older partner is right - he keeps the protected award. The advice to
claim Carer's Allowance for underlying entitlement is right but **mistimed**: there is an earnings
limit, so it fails while she is working. The claim to lodge is the one that starts the day she
retires.

**Why it needs you.** All four are phone calls and logins. None of them can be answered from the
repo, and three cards are waiting on the answers.
