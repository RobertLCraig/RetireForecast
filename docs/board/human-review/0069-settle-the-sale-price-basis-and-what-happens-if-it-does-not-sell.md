# Settle what the flat is worth, and what happens if it does not sell

## What I need from you

**Three things.**

1. Instruct a formal RICS valuation. Not another agent's appraisal.
2. Get the lender's position in writing: what happens at the redemption date if the flat is not
   under offer, and what evidence they need for any extension.
3. Tell me which sale price the base scenario should run on.

---

**Why.** Every sell plan balances on one number, and the evidence for that number spans a very wide
range - an agent's desktop appraisal at the top, a broker working well below it, and one lender's
surveyor declining to lend at all. The flat has been on the market for months without an offer,
which has already falsified the top of that range.

The property reviewer's advice, in his words: discard the highest appraisal entirely - it came from
the agent who wanted the instruction, it was never repeated, and the market has answered it. Keep
the middle figure as the properly-marketed, willing-seller price. Then add a **lower**
restricted-marketing figure and run the base on that, because a seller against a redemption
deadline is not a willing seller. That distinction is the standard valuation one and it is the one
that applies here.

A formal valuation costs a few hundred pounds. On a block where a surveyor has already declined to
lend, it is worth more than three more agents' opinions, and it is the document a lender will
actually respond to.

**On 2.** This is the largest real-world risk in the whole file and the model cannot express it at
all. Every scenario silently assumes the refinance or the sale completes. If neither does, the
account goes to redemption in default, and there is no scenario for that. The reviewer notes the
lender will normally consider an extension only on evidence of a sale agreed with an estimated
completion date - so there is a point in the autumn after which no bridge exists.

**Pass** is all three of:
- a written RICS valuation
- a written lender position covering the no-sale case
- a decision from you on which price the base runs on

**Fail** is another verbal appraisal, or a lender answer given over the phone with nothing in
writing.

**Why it needs you.** These are instructions to third parties and a judgement about which price to
plan on. Card 0027 needs the forced-sale discount anchored to something real, and no code change
can settle a valuation.
