# No partly-annuitised what-if is offered to compare

## Why
Card 0060 let an annuity be bought from a named non-pension account, so a reader can now build a
partly-annuitised plan by hand on a what-if child. Its fourth Task, a ready-made
partly-annuitised what-if variant, was left open: a preset has to choose HOW MUCH of the savings
to annuitise and at what age, and that is the adviser's question, not a default the engine may
supply (no invisible figures). The expert panel's point (adviser finding 3, 2026-08-19) was that
a comparison between two plans without the partly-annuitised third option is incomplete. Raised
by the 0060 scope review and the 2026-09-28 manager pass, which found no card carried it.
